About Nepal College of Information Technology

Nepal College of Information Technology (NCIT) is renowned for its excellence in IT and engineering education. Established with the mission to cultivate industry-ready graduates, NCIT has been at the forefront of technology education in Nepal.

Why NCIT?

  • Wide range of IT and engineering programs.
  • Robust internship opportunities and job placements.
  • Active student-led research initiatives.
  • Global collaborations and industry partnerships.

NCIT continues to shape the future of technology-driven education, empowering students with the skills needed in the digital era.
